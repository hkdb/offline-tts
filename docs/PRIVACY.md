# Privacy Policy for OfflineTTS

**Last updated:** November 29, 2025

## Overview

OfflineTTS is designed with privacy as a core principle. The extension performs all text-to-speech processing locally on your device.

## Data Collection

**We do not collect any data.**

- No personal information is collected
- No browsing history is tracked
- No text content is transmitted to external servers
- No analytics or telemetry is collected
- No cookies are used

## How It Works

All TTS processing happens entirely on your device using local ONNX models. The text you read never leaves your browser.

## Permissions

The extension requests the following permissions:

- **activeTab**: To read text content from the current page when you activate the extension
- **storage**: To save your preferences (voice selection, speed) locally in your browser
- **contextMenus**: To add "Read with OfflineTTS" to the right-click menu
- **offscreen**: To run the TTS engine in a background document for audio playback

## Third-Party Services

This extension does not communicate with any third-party services.

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/hkdb/offline-tts).

## Changes

Any changes to this privacy policy will be posted in the GitHub repository.
