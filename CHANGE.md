## [v0.01.00] - 2025-11-29

Initial public release of OfflineTTS.

- 4 natural TTS voices (M1, M2, F1, F2)
- Read full pages or selected text
- PDF support
- Adjustable speed (0.5x - 2.0x)
- Keyboard shortcuts (Alt+P, Alt+O, Alt+., Alt+,)
- Fully offline - all processing done locally
